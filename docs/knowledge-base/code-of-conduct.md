# ASTA Code of Conduct

Source: `knowledge source/🧘 Stock market sanskar Journal/ASTA Code of Conduct PDF.pdf` (Avadhut Sathe Trading Academy). The app reads these lines as the daily sanskar oath. Each line is tagged with the primary virtue it trains ([virtues-measurement.md](../specifications/virtues-measurement.md)).

| # | Affirmation | Trains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Market is God and I am very grateful to Market for giving opportunities to succeed. | Focus |
| 2 | I follow the market price action and not my mind. | Focus |
| 3 | I trade only high probability setups. | Patience |
| 4 | I always mind my entry. | Discipline |
| 5 | I analyse charts as if I have no position in them. | Focus |
| 6 | I consistently follow my trading plans. | Discipline |
| 7 | I am accountable for my trading. | Discipline |
| 8 | I keep my trading diary with performance matrix. | Discipline |
| 9 | I am always with the dominant force in the market. | Patience |
| 10 | I believe in my trading strategies completely and whole heartedly. | Discipline |
| 11 | I always follow what the market is doing and not what I want market to do. | Patience |
| 12 | I practice proper risk management, I let the profit run and take only small losses. | Discipline |
| 13 | I take only those trades that give me reward which clearly outweighs the risk. | Patience |
| 14 | I find other things to do once my trades go live. | Focus |
| 15 | I am not emotionally affected by profits/losses. | Focus |
| 16 | I count my profit only after exiting my trades. | Patience |
| 17 | I continue to stay in my trades till I find a good cause to exit. | Patience |
| 18 | I am happy and satisfied with every profit. | Patience |
| 19 | I respect money and I always protect my capital. | Discipline |
| 20 | I am detached from news and fundamentals while trading. | Focus |
| 21 | I am the most disciplined trader ever. | Discipline |
| 22 | I am happy and a successful trader. | Focus |

The PDF repeats line 1 near the end; it is listed once here.
