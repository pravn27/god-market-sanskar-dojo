# User workflows

## Onboarding (once)

1. Import the existing sanskar journal spreadsheets (optional) to seed history.
2. Set capital, weekly brick size, risk range, and pick a season (for example the 50-trade challenge).
3. Review the daily rituals, the Code of Conduct, and the two consequence rules (Red Zone, capital lock).

## Morning (8:00 to 9:15 AM, market days)

1. Open the app and read the day's sanskar line from the Code of Conduct.
2. Tick rituals as they are done; the mood level rises with each ritual.
3. Walk the Market Overview checklist: global indices, Indian indices, bond-risk thresholds, sector relative performance, FII/DII/Pro/Client sentiment, commodities.
4. The readiness gate totals preparation into a score; 80 or more unlocks the Trade Gate.

## Trade (market hours)

1. Draft a plan at the Trade Gate: setup, weapon candle, entry, stop loss, quantity.
2. The app computes risk and blocks the plan if it is outside the weekly risk band.
3. Accept the exact rupee loss before the plan is logged.
4. Place the order with the broker (outside the app) and record the stop-loss proof.

## Close (after each trade and at 3:30 PM)

1. Complete the audit: stop loss set, moved, manual exit, exit reason, emotion tag, one lesson.
2. The app assigns Grade A or F and an outcome quadrant.
3. At 3:30 PM the scorecard shows Discipline %, applies any lock, and writes XP events.

## Weekly (Saturday or Sunday)

1. Rate the five roles (Opportunity Hunter, Opportunity Converter, Risk Manager, Money Manager, Reviewer and Auditor) from 1 to 5.
2. Record screen time, average profit to loss ratio, mood, and one improvement.
3. Set next week's focus: capital, brick size, risk range, opportunities, execution strategy.

## Monthly (month end)

1. Set or review the monthly theme and answer "did it work?".
2. Confirm or edit the SWOT drafted from the month's quadrant counts.
3. Review the improvement verdict and level progress.
